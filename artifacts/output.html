# Nutrition Plan for Marathon Training

## Daily Calorie & Macronutrient Targets

**Estimated Daily Needs:** 2,800–3,200 calories (depending on training intensity)

**Macronutrient Distribution:**
- **Carbohydrates: 55-65%** (385–520g) — prioritize for endurance fuel
- **Protein: 12-15%** (84–120g) — muscle repair and recovery
- **Fat: 20-25%** (62–89g) — hormone function and satiety

---

## Pre-Carb Loading (3-5 Days Before Race)

Increase carbs to **70% of calories** while reducing fiber slightly:
- White rice, pasta, bread, potatoes
- Low-fiber fruits (bananas, berries, melon)
- Honey, jam, sports drinks
- Lean proteins and minimal fat

---

## Daily Meal Framework

### **Breakfast**
- Oatmeal with banana, berries, honey, and low-fat yogurt
- Whole grain toast with almond butter
- Orange juice or sports drink

### **Mid-Morning Snack**
- Sports drink (6-8% carbs) + banana or energy bar

### **Lunch**
- Lean protein (chicken/fish): 150g
- Brown rice or pasta: 2 cups cooked
- Vegetables (broccoli, carrots): moderate portions
- Olive oil for cooking

### **Pre-Training Snack** (1-2 hours before)
- Bagel with honey
- Sports drink or energy gel

### **Post-Training** (within 30 min)
- Chocolate milk, smoothie, or recovery drink
- Carbs + protein ratio: 3-4:1

### **Dinner**
- Lean meat/fish: 150g
- Sweet potato or quinoa: 1.5 cups
- Leafy greens with light dressing
- Whole grain bread

### **Evening Snack** (optional)
- Greek yogurt with granola
- Whole grain cereal with milk

---

## Hydration
- **Daily:** 3-4L water minimum
- **During long runs (>90 min):** 500-1000mL sports drink per hour
- **Electrolytes:** especially sodium for sweat replacement

---

## Key Tips
✓ Eat familiar foods during training (test everything on race day)  
✓ Time meals 2-3 hours before intense sessions  
✓ Prioritize whole grains for sustained energy  
✓ Monitor energy levels and adjust portions accordingly
